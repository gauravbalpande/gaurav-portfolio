~/about

About me

I'm a final-year Computer Science student focused on Cloud Computing, DevOps, Platform Engineering, and MLOps. Over the last few months I've been building production-style cloud-native applications on AWS, automating infrastructure with Terraform, and running everything through Kubernetes and GitOps pipelines with ArgoCD.

Lately I've also picked up Go — writing backend services with Gin, JWT auth, and a clean repository/service-layer architecture — and used it to build my flagship project, AuctionXI. On the side, I'm exploring MLOps (MLflow, DVC, Prometheus/Grafana) and the basics of LLMOps — RAG systems and containerized AI workloads.

What I enjoy most is the systems side of engineering: designing infrastructure that's reproducible, automating the boring parts of deployment, and building services that hold up under real traffic.
